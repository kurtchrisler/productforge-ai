ProductGenie AI — One purchase email can no longer activate more than one account
====================================================================================

What this does
---------------
Up to now, any account could type any purchase email into "Refresh my
license" in Settings, and the app would grant that account whatever tier
the license server said the email was entitled to — with no check for
whether another account had already claimed that same email. That meant
one purchase email could unlock Standard/Pro (or KDP Accelerator) on as
many different accounts as someone tried it on.

Now, before granting access from a license-server "yes", the app checks
whether another account already actively holds that email (an active
Standard/Pro tier, or the KDP Accelerator add-on). If so, the new attempt
is blocked with a clear message ("This purchase email is already active on
a different ProductGenie AI account...") and nothing changes for either
account — the existing holder keeps their access untouched, and the
account making the blocked attempt just doesn't get granted anything.

This is checked in both places a license gets verified:
- Settings -> "Refresh my license"
- The automatic check that runs right after signup (in case someone signs
  up using an email that's already bound to a different account as a
  purchase email)

An account can always re-verify the email already bound to ITSELF (e.g.
clicking "Refresh my license" again, or after later buying KDP
Accelerator) — this only blocks a DIFFERENT account from claiming an
already-claimed email.

I tested this against a stub license server (so I could control its
response without touching your real WP Marketer Tools server) covering:
one account claiming an email, a second account being blocked from the
same email (with its own existing access left untouched), an account
freely re-verifying its own email, and a brand-new signup being blocked
when its login email happens to match an email already claimed elsewhere.
All passed.

Nothing to backfill — this only changes what happens on the NEXT license
check for any account, it doesn't touch existing data. If two accounts
already share the same license_email from before this fix, both will keep
their current access; the block only kicks in the next time either of them
(or a third account) tries to activate that email going forward, at which
point whichever one is NOT already holding real access will be the one
blocked.
